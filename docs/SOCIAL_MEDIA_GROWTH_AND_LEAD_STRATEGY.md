## 2. Content engine (short-form first: Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels — same 30–45 s video, 3 platforms)
Weekly cadence a 1-person team can keep: **3 short videos + 2 stories + 1 carousel.**
| Series | Format | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Before → After | 15–30 s, split screen, real job (customer consent) | "2 BHK kitchen, Kharadi — 4 hours" |
| How we do it | 30–45 s process clip | Sofa shampoo & extraction; termite drilling & injection (safety gear visible) |
| Myth vs fact | Talking head / text | "Does pest control smell for days?" — only factual claims |
| Team | Staff intro, uniform, training | Builds the "in-house staff" trust message |
| Customer voice | 10–20 s testimonial (written consent) | Real customers only; never scripted reviews |
| Local | Society/office jobs by area | Hadapsar, Magarpatta, Kharadi, Wagholi, Undri |

Rules: real footage only; no customer faces/addresses without written consent; captions on every video (most watch
muted); end card "Book on booking.saharacleaningservices.com — Pay after service"; no prices in evergreen videos.

## 3. Turning views into leads (measured in the CRM)
- Every link carries UTMs: `utm_source=instagram|youtube|facebook&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=<series>`; the CRM
  stores them on the lead (Marketing report → filter by source).
- Pin one Reel per service with the booking link; Instagram Highlights: Reviews, Before/After, Pest, Painting, Offices.
- YouTube: one long video per service (3–5 min) titled with the search phrase ("Home deep cleaning in Pune — full
  process"), description with booking link and phone; Shorts link to it.
- Reply to every comment/DM within the working day; DMs asking for prices → booking link + call/WhatsApp.
- WhatsApp: viewers who message first may be asked for marketing opt-in inside the 24-hour window (consent recorded).
