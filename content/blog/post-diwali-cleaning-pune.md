## 4 Critical Areas Needing Post-Festive Deep Cleaning

### 1. Kitchen Degreasing

Festive cooking means hours of frying and sweet-making. Oil vapour settles on the chimney, hob, tiles, cabinets and exhaust fan, and it hardens into sticky grease if left for weeks.

- Chimney and filter degreasing
- Hob, slab and backsplash tile cleaning
- Cabinet exteriors, sink and exhaust fan

### 2. Sofa and Carpet Foam Washing

Guests, snacks and sweets mean stains, spills and trapped dust in your upholstery and rugs.

- Foam / shampoo wash for fabric sofas and carpets
- Stain treatment for food, oil and drink spills
- Odour removal and a fresher, hygienic finish

### 3. Floor Scrubbing

Rangoli colours, diya oil, footfall and crackers leave floors dull and stained. Mopping alone cannot remove it.

- Machine scrubbing for marble, vitrified tiles and granite
- Grout and corner cleaning
- Anti-skid, shine-restoring finish

### 4. Balcony Pressure Washing

Fireworks soot, ash and dust collect on balcony floors, grills and railings.

- High-pressure jet wash for floors and walls
- Grill and railing cleaning
- Drain and corner cleaning

> 💡 **Pro tip:** Start with the balcony and kitchen, then do sofas, and finish with floors. This way dirt never settles on a surface you have already cleaned.
